Welcome to Noor Al-Banat

We provide comprehensive courses to enrich your understanding and practice of Islam.

About Noor Al-Banat

Our Vision

To educate and empower young girls through Islamic knowledge, fostering a deeper connection with their faith.

Our Mission

Offering structured courses that cover Quranic recitation, Tajweed, Hifz, Namaz, Ahadees, Noorani Qaida, Masnoon Duas, Six Kalmas, and Tafseer of Quran.

Our Values

We are dedicated to providing a supportive and inclusive environment for all students, encouraging growth and spiritual development.

Courses

Quran Recitation - تلاوة القرآن

Learn the proper way to recite the Quran with correct pronunciation and intonation.

Tajweed - تجويد

Enhance your Quran recitation with accurate rules and techniques.

Hifz - حفظ

Memorize the Quran efficiently with our structured Hifz program tailored for girls.

Complete Namaz - صلاة كاملة

Learn the complete method of performing Salah, including all the essential prayers and movements.

Ahadees - الأحاديث

Study the sayings and traditions of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and incorporate them into your life.

Noorani Qaida - نوراني قاعدة

Start your Quranic journey with Noorani Qaida, perfect for beginners to learn the Arabic alphabet and pronunciation.

Masnoon Duas - مسنون دعاؤں

Learn the daily supplications (Duas) prescribed by the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) for various occasions.

Six Kalmas - شش کلمے

Memorize and understand the six Kalmas, which are essential declarations of faith in Islam.

Tarjma Tafseer - ترجمہ تفسیر

Delve deeper into the meanings of the Quranic verses with comprehensive translation and Tafseer.
